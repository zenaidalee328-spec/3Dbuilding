# Windcatcher: facts to check before posting

- "Forty five degrees out here": summer highs in Yazd and central Iran reach the low-to-mid 40s °C. Check a weather source.
- Hook thermometer: OUTSIDE 45 °C / INSIDE 29 °C is an illustration, not a measured value. Find a source for the
  temperature drop of a windcatcher + water (studies on Yazd badgirs and qanats), or soften the number.
- The water: many houses used a pool or a qanat (underground channel) in the basement; air passing over it is cooled
  by evaporation and the cold water. The film simplifies this to one channel under the floor.
- "In Iran, towers like this have cooled homes for hundreds of years": Yazd is known as the city of windcatchers
  (badgirs); check a source for the age.

# Quicksand: facts to check before posting
- Quicksand density ~2 g/cm³ vs the human body ~1 g/cm³: you cannot fully sink; people sink to about the waist
  (Bonn, Nature 2005: "Quicksand and the human body").
- Shaking/struggling liquefies it; you sink faster, then it packs hard around the legs.
- Pulling a foot out at 1 cm/s needs a force of ~10^6 N in the lab study, quoted as "like lifting a medium-sized car".
- Escape advice (lean back, spread out, wiggle legs slowly to let water in, move slowly toward firm ground): common
  survival guidance (e.g. National Geographic, survival manuals).

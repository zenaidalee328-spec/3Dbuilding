# 10,000 years: facts to check before posting
- The place is a made-up river bend "in the Indus valley": a reconstruction of a typical history, not one real site.
- Farming in the region from ~7000 BC (Mehrgarh); the film says "six thousand BC" for the first farmers at this spot.
- Indus (Harappan) cities ~2600-1900 BC: baked bricks, grid streets, covered drains (Mohenjo-daro, Harappa).
- Decline ~1900 BC; river shifts are one of the proposed causes. Mounds of later towns often sit on older ruins.
- "Four and a half thousand years of history": from ~2500 BC to today.

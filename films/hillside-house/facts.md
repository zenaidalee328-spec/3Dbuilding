# Hillside House: facts to verify before posting

Script: the owner's own (2026-11-23), trimmed to ~75 s by Claude.
- Pilotis: columns that lift a building off the ground (Le Corbusier's term). On slopes they level the floor without cutting the hill.
- Crest (interceptor) channel: a ditch across the slope above a cut/building that catches runoff and leads it sideways.
- Hydraulic staircase (stepped spillway / cascade): stepped concrete channels dissipate the water's energy on slopes and roadside cuts.
- Soil creep: slow downslope movement of the top soil; deep foundations (piles) carry loads down to rock below the moving layer.
- Soil nailing: steel bars grouted into drilled holes + a sprayed concrete (shotcrete) facing.
- Deep (horizontal) drains: perforated pipes drilled into a slope to lower the water pressure behind it.
- Rockfall barrier nets: flexible steel nets on posts that catch falling rocks.
- Booster pump + elevated tank; inclined elevator (cable-pulled cart on rails) for hillside homes.

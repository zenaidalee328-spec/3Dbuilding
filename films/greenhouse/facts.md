- Polycarbonate covering: clear twin-wall sheets that let light in and resist hail.
- Bracing: steel arches tied with diagonal (X) braces; feet set in concrete blocks against wind uplift.
- Thermostat: a sensor switches the heater on below a set temperature and off above it.
- Greenhouse effect inside a closed house / a closed car: light comes in, heat is trapped.
- Skylights / roof and side vents: hot air leaves at the top, cool air comes in low (stack ventilation).
- Drip irrigation: perforated / dripper hoses water the roots directly. Drainage: excess water led out.
- Fertigation: fertilizer dosed into the irrigation water.
- Insect screen: fine mesh over vents (anti-insect nets).
- Circulation (HAF) fans keep the air moving so leaves dry and mould is less likely.
- Sectors with a fan-and-pad (wet wall) evaporative cooling system.
- Tomatoes self-pollinate; growers vibrate the flower clusters (electric pollination wands) to release pollen.
- Stingless bees (meliponines) are used to pollinate crops such as strawberries in greenhouses.

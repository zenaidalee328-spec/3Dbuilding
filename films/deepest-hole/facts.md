# Deepest hole: facts to check before posting
- Kola Superdeep Borehole (SG-3), Kola Peninsula, Russia: 12,262 m deep (reached 1989), drilling from 1970: "almost twenty years".
- Borehole diameter ~23 cm (9 in). The site is now a welded/bolted metal cap.
- Bottom temperature ~180 °C; the rock behaved more like plastic than solid rock at depth (reported by the Kola team).
- Eiffel Tower 330 m; Burj Khalifa 828 m; Titanic wreck ~3,800 m; deepest mine (Mponeng, South Africa) ~4 km;
  Everest 8,849 m; Challenger Deep (Mariana Trench) ~10,935 m. Earth's radius 6,371 km: 12.262 / 6,371 = 0.19 %.
- Everest's shape is stylised (narrower than the real mountain); everything else is to scale.

# Earthquake test: facts to check before posting
- An illustration, not a simulation: the same shaking under three typical building types.
- Adobe (mud brick) and unreinforced masonry are the most vulnerable buildings in earthquakes: heavy, brittle, weak in tension.
- Base isolation (rubber/lead-rubber bearings) decouples a building from the ground's motion; used for hospitals and
  airports (e.g. Istanbul Sabiha Gökçen terminal, one of the largest base-isolated buildings).
- Magnitude is used loosely for "stronger shaking"; real damage depends on distance, depth and ground.

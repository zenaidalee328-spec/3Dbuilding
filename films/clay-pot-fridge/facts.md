# Facts to check before posting

- **"A pot like this can stay more than ten degrees cooler than the air around it."** Pot-in-pot coolers (zeer) are
  widely reported to cool their inside by roughly 10-15 °C in hot, dry air. Find and link one source you trust (for
  example a university extension or Practical Action report) and change the number in timeline.js if it says otherwise.
- **"Tomatoes that rotted in two days can last for weeks."** Reports on pot-in-pot coolers in Nigeria say tomatoes kept
  about 3 weeks instead of 2-3 days. Check a source before posting.
- **Physics:** water takes heat (latent heat) from the wet clay when it evaporates, so the wall, the sand and the inner
  pot get colder. It works best in dry, moving air, which is why the pot sits in the shade where the wind reaches it.
